# Community conduct

Help people learn. Be respectful of differences in experience, language, identity, and background. Critique explanations and implementations with evidence, not personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately misleading teaching material are not acceptable. Respect the privacy of learners and contributors. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community.

贡献讨论以事实和学习效果为依据，尊重不同经验与语言背景。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、泄露隐私和故意传播错误教材。维护者可以移除有害内容并限制相关参与。
